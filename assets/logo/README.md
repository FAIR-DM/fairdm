# FairDM logo

These are the official FairDM logo files. Every file is an SVG of outlined vector shapes, so it needs no fonts and scales to any size. Export PNG or ICO from these files when you need them; exports are not kept in the repository.

The mark is the F drawn as FairDM's core model: a project holding a dataset holding a record.

## Who may use the logo

**The FairDM project** uses every file here for any purpose.

**Anyone may use the unmodified logo to refer to FairDM**, without asking first. That includes:

- a portal built on FairDM saying so, for example "Built with FairDM" in a footer or an about page
- documentation, tutorials, talks, posters, papers and teaching material about FairDM
- contributors' and integrators' own sites, when linking to the project
- news and reviews

**Ask first** (open an issue on [FAIR-DM/fairdm](https://github.com/FAIR-DM/fairdm/issues)) before:

- using the logo as the logo of your own portal, organisation, product or service
- putting it on anything you sell, including merchandise
- using it in a way that suggests FairDM endorses, certifies or is run by you

**Never** modify the logo, combine it with another mark, or use it for something unrelated to FairDM.

The MIT licence covers FairDM's code. It does not cover these logo files or the FairDM name, which are governed by this page.

## Which file to use

**Horizontal** (`fairdm-horizontal`):

The primary logo, with the descriptor "FAIR Data Management". Use it wherever there is room.

**Compact** (`fairdm-horizontal-compact`):

The horizontal logo without the descriptor, for headers, navbars and anywhere under 56 px tall.

**Stacked** (`fairdm-stacked`):

For square spaces, title pages and posters.

**Symbol** (`fairdm-symbol`):

The mark alone, once the name has already appeared nearby. `-square` is padded for avatars and social profiles, `-favicon` fills its square for browser tabs, and `-app-icon` is the white mark on a prussian rounded tile.

Every logo comes in these versions:

- **no suffix**: full colour, for light backgrounds
- **`-dark`**: full colour, for dark backgrounds
- **`-black`**, **`-white`**, **`-mono-17517d`**: a single colour, for anything that cannot carry two colours, such as single-ink print, embroidery, stamps or overlays on photographs

## Rules

**Clear space:**

Keep a clear space on every side equal to the side of the small square in the mark, one quarter of the mark's height. Nothing else enters that space, including the edge of a container.

**Minimum size:**

Symbol 16 px. Compact 20 px tall. Horizontal 56 px tall on screen and 15 mm in print. Stacked 96 px tall.

**Colour:**

- prussian `#17517D`: the mark
- ink `#111820`: the wordmark
- ink soft `#414C58`: the descriptor
- on dark backgrounds, the mark becomes `#4A86BB` and the wordmark `#E8ECF0`

On a prussian or busy background, use the white version.

**Don't:**

Stretch or squash the logo, recolour it, rotate or reflect it, add shadows or effects, retype the wordmark in another font, or put the full-colour version on prussian.
