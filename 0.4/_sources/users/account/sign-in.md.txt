# Sign in

The Sign in screen is where you prove who you are, so the portal can show you your private records and let you edit the ones you may.

You reach it by selecting **Sign in** on the [Home](../home.md) screen, or by selecting **Sign in** on the [Sign up](sign-up.md) screen. A screen that needs you to be signed in sends you here and brings you back to it afterwards. The address is `account-center/login/`. When no screen sent you here, you arrive on the [Home](../home.md) screen after you sign in, headed **My Dashboard**.

On a wide screen the page is split in two. One half holds the form. The other half names the portal, with the line `Research data portal` above its name, then its description and `Run by` followed by the organization that runs it. You do not need this half to sign in. On a narrow screen only the form shows.

## The form

A card headed **Sign in**, with the portal's logo above the heading. It holds:

- **Email**, where you type the address of your account
- **Password**, with the link **Forgot your password?** under it
- **Remember Me**, a checkbox
- the **Sign In** button

When the email address and password do not match, the screen says that the email address and/or password you specified are not correct.

## The ORCID button

Below the form, after a line reading **or**, is an **ORCID** button. Selecting it opens a page headed **Sign In Via ORCID**, where you select **Continue** to go to ORCID and sign in with your ORCID iD.

If a profile with your ORCID iD already exists and nobody has claimed it, signing in this way also claims it. See [How to claim your profile with ORCID](claim-your-profile-with-orcid.md).

## The link to Sign up

At the foot of the card: **No account yet?** with the link **Create one**.

## When you are already signed in

A signed-in user who opens the address is sent on to the home screen instead of seeing the form.

## What you can do here

- Make an account. See [How to create an account](create-an-account.md).
- Take over a profile made for you. See [How to claim your profile with ORCID](claim-your-profile-with-orcid.md).
- Find out why a profile did not claim. See [How to fix a profile that will not claim](fix-a-profile-that-will-not-claim.md).
