# How to edit an organization's profile

This guide changes what an organization's public page says: its name, logo, type, place, description and web addresses. The people who keep the organization's record do it from the portal, without asking an administrator.

## Before you start

You must be signed in, and be one of these:

- the organization's owner or one of its administrators, with an affiliation that has not ended
- a Community Manager

An ordinary member and a person with no affiliation cannot open the editing screen. Only the organization's owner and administrators see its checklist.

## Steps

1. Open the organization from the [Organizations](../community/organizations.md) screen, by selecting its name.
2. Open the **Manage** menu in the header and choose **Edit details**. A Community Manager who does not keep the organization selects the **Edit details** button instead.
3. Change the fields you want to change. The [Edit organization](edit-organization.md) screen lists them all.
4. To put the organization under another, search for it in **Part of**. To make it part of nothing, clear the box.
5. To add web addresses, type the organization's own in **Website** and any others one on each line of **Other links**. Each begins `http://` or `https://`.
6. Select **Save**.

## The result

You return to the organization's page with the message **Your changes were saved.** A changed name or logo shows wherever the organization is named, on the records it owns and on the profiles of its members. The **Organization record** checklist counts the items that are in place. See [Organization](organization.md).

If a field cannot be saved, nothing is saved. The field says what is wrong, and everything else you typed stays in the form. Correct the field and select **Save** again. Select **Cancel** to leave without changing anything.
