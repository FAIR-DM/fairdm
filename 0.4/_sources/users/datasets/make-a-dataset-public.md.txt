# How to make a dataset public

Making a dataset public lets anyone, signed in or not, open its page and read its description, dates, identifiers and counts. Do it when you are ready to tell the community what the dataset is. Editing stays with the people you list on it.

## Before you start

You need the **Can manage** level on the dataset or on the project above it. Only managers see the **Visibility** field. See [How access to a record is decided](../../explanation/how-access-to-a-record-is-decided.md).

## Steps

1. Open the dataset's **Contributors** tab. Check that only people you trust hold **Can edit** or **Can manage**. A public dataset keeps its levels, so these people can still change it.
2. Open the **Manage** menu on [the dataset page](dataset.md) and choose **Edit details**.
3. Under **Visibility**, choose **Public**.
4. Select **Save & continue**.
5. Open the dataset in a private browser window. The page should show its details and offer no editing controls.

## The result

The dataset's page opens to everyone. It appears on the [Datasets](datasets.md) screen, and the **Private** badge and the private-dataset notice are gone. The badge reads **Unpublished** until the dataset is published.

Making a dataset public does not release its samples and measurements. They stay hidden until the dataset is published, and the page tells visitors so. Publication is a separate step that a researcher cannot take from a portal screen. See [Dataset publication, DOIs and DataCite metadata](../../explanation/dataset-publication-dois-and-datacite-metadata.md).

When the dataset sits in a private project, visitors can open the dataset but do not see the project named or linked. Make the project public on its [Edit details](../projects/edit-details.md) screen if you want it shown.
