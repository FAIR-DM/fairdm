# Planning notes: capture, store and query locations as geospatial data

Suggestions from the maintainer for research and planning. The plan answers each one by name.

## Survey the available packages

"Please do your own research on available packages." Before anything is written by hand, look at
what GeoDjango and published packages already give for each part of this feature: reading
coordinates in several notations, converting from EPSG reference systems, a form input for a
location, GeoJSON in the API, location columns in tabular import and export, and spatial coverage
across the 180th meridian.
