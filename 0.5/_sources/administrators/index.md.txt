# Administrators

This section is for you if you run a FairDM portal that already exists: you look after its people
and roles, review its content and keep its configuration sound, mostly through the administration
interface. Each page is one of three kinds. How-to guides take you through one task, reference pages
describe an administration screen or a set of rules, and the explanation covers how a part of the
portal behaves.
