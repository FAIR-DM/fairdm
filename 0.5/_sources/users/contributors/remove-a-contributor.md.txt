# How to remove a contributor

Removing a contributor takes them off the record's credits. Do it when someone was credited by mistake, or when a person should no longer have the access their credit gives.

## Before you start

You need to manage the record, which means holding the **Can manage** level on it or on the record above it.

Two things can stop a removal:

- A record always keeps a manager. The portal refuses to remove the only person who can manage it. Give someone else **Can manage** first, as described in [How to let a colleague into a private record](let-a-colleague-into-a-private-record.md).
- An organization cannot be removed while anyone on the record is credited from it. [Change their organization](change-the-organization-a-person-is-credited-from.md) or remove them first.

## Steps

1. Open the record's page and choose its **Contributors** tab.
2. Choose **Remove** beside the person, or open the menu on the organization's card and choose **Remove**. You can also choose **Remove from this record** on the **Edit a contributor** screen.
3. Read the screen. It asks "Remove" followed by the name and "from this" and the record's kind, and lists what the removal does.
4. Choose **Remove**.

To change your mind, choose **Cancel**.

## The result

The contributor is no longer credited on the record or named in its citation. A person loses the access they held through being listed on it. If they hold a level on a record above, they keep that, and it is changed on that record's own Contributors tab.

An organization a person was credited from stays under Organizations. Remove it separately if it should go too.

If you add the person again later, they start at **Can view** with no roles, and you are asked for their organization again.

The screen is described on [Contributors](contributors-tab.md). For why a record keeps a manager, see [How access to a record is decided](../../explanation/how-access-to-a-record-is-decided.md).
