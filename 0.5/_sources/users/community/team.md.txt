# Portal Team

The Portal Team screen names the people who run the portal. It lists the holders of each portal role, so a visitor can see who maintains the portal and whom to ask about it.

You reach it by opening **Community** in the navigation menu and choosing **Team**. Anyone can open it, signed in or not. The menu calls it **Team**, and the screen's heading is **Portal Team**.

## The heading

Under the heading **Portal Team** is the line **Meet the individuals that maintain this portal and keep the community thriving.** An information button beside the heading opens a short note about why a portal needs a team, with an **About portal roles** link to the FairDM site. The link opens in a new tab.

## The roles

The rest of the screen has one section for each portal role that somebody holds. A section has the role's name as its heading and, under it, one card for each person who holds the role, four across on a wide screen. The roles come in a fixed order, and a role nobody holds is left out:

1. **Portal Administrator**
2. **Data Curator**
3. **Community Manager**
4. **Developer**

A person who holds more than one role appears under each. A person whose account is deactivated is left out.

A card is the same one the [People](people.md) screen shows: the person's photo or initials, their name linked to their profile with an ORCID icon when they have an ORCID iD, their primary organization and its place, and a badge for each portal role they hold.

Only portal roles appear here. The roles people hold on a record, such as creator or contact person, do not. See [Portal roles](../../administrators/reference/portal-roles.md) for what each role allows.

## When it is empty

When nobody holds any portal role, the screen shows its heading and the line under it, and no sections.

## What you can do here

- Open a person's profile by selecting their name. See [Your profile](../account/your-profile.md) for what a profile shows.
- Find everyone else in the portal. See [People](people.md).
