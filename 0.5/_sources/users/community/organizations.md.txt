# Organizations

The Organizations screen lists every organization the portal knows: the institutions, funders, facilities and companies that people on the portal belong to or that records credit. It is the place to find an organization's page.

You reach it by opening **Community** in the navigation menu and choosing **Organizations**, or by selecting **Organizations** in the **Community** section of the [Home](../home.md) screen. Anyone can open it, signed in or not.

## The toolbar

Along the top of the list are:

- the heading **Organizations**, with a count such as `8 organizations`
- a search box headed **Name**, with a **Search** button
- a **Country** text box
- a **Filters** button

Below the toolbar, each search or filter you have applied shows as a button. Selecting one removes it. When you have applied more than one, **Clear all** removes them together. Once a search or filter is applied, the count reads `n results` or **No results**.

## The filters panel

The **Filters** button opens a panel beside the list with one text box, **City**. The panel has an **Apply** button, and a **Clear** button once a filter in it is set.

## The cards

Each organization is a card. Cards are laid out two across on a medium screen and three across on a wide one. A card shows:

- the organization's logo, or its initials when it has no logo
- its name, linked to its page, followed by a ROR icon when it has a ROR identifier
- a badge for its type, such as **Education** or **Funder**, when it has one
- its city and country, when it has them
- a badge with its number of members, such as `3 members`
- a badge for each kind of record it is credited on, with a count
- the line **No members or credits yet** when it has neither

The list shows 25 organizations to a page. When there are more, the foot of the screen shows `Showing` a range `of` the total, with page numbers. On a narrow screen it shows two buttons and the page count instead.

## When it is empty

A portal with no organizations shows an icon and the heading **There's nothing here yet**. The screen has no button for adding an organization.

When a search or filter matches nothing, the heading reads **No organizations match**, with the line **Try other words or fewer filters.** and a **Clear all** button.

## What you can do here

- Open an organization by selecting its name. See [Organization](../account/organization.md) for what its page shows.
- Change an organization's details when you keep its record. See [How to edit an organization's profile](../account/edit-an-organizations-profile.md).
- Credit an organization on a record you manage. See [How to add a person or organization](../contributors/add-a-person-or-organization.md).
