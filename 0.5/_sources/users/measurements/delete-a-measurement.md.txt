# How to delete a measurement

Deleting a measurement removes it from the portal for good, together with its descriptions, dates and identifiers. Do it when a measurement was recorded by mistake or should no longer be held. The sample it was made on is not touched.

## Before you start

You need to be able to manage the measurement, which means holding the **Can manage** level on it or on the record above it. Someone who can only edit the measurement does not see **Delete**. See [How access to a record is decided](../../explanation/how-access-to-a-record-is-decided.md).

Deletion cannot be undone.

## Steps

1. Open the measurement's page and choose **Manage**.
2. Choose **Delete**, the last entry, set apart from the others by a divider.
3. Read what the page lists under "The following related records will also be permanently deleted:". Those rows are deleted with the measurement.
4. Type the measurement's name in the field headed "Type the name to confirm". A measurement with no name is confirmed by typing its portal ID instead, and the page shows you which. It must match exactly. The **Delete** button stays off until it does.
5. Choose **Delete**.

To change your mind, choose **Back**. It returns you to the measurement's page.

## The result

The measurement is gone. You arrive on the page of the dataset it belonged to, or on the datasets list if you may not open that dataset, and a message reads "Deleted" followed by the measurement's name or portal ID.

The screen itself is described on [Delete](../editing-a-record/delete.md).
