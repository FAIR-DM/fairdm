A reuser asks four things of a single measurement: what the result was, how it was measured, what it was measured on, and whether they can cite and reuse it. The page answers them in that order. The anatomy and the facts column come from `overview/page.html`; the context is gathered by `fairdm/core/measurement/overview.py`.
