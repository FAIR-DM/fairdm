{% extends "overview/page.html" %} {% load i18n humanize fairdm thumbnail %} {% comment %} The measurement's own page, drawn from the base Measurement model and what the registry says about the measurement's type. A reuser asks four things of a single measurement: what the result was, how it was measured, what it was measured on, and whether they can cite and reuse it. The result is the header's headline. The content answers the other three in order. The anatomy comes from `overview/page.html` and the context is gathered by the measurement `Overview` plugin. A measurement type adds its own content by providing `/_overview.html`, which extends this template and fills its blocks: {% extends "measurement/measurement_overview.html" %} {% block overview.result %}{% endblock %} {% block overview.properties %}{% endblock %} Measurement blocks: overview.result (along the header's foot, shown when the type declares a `value`, otherwise the block a type fills first), then in the wide column overview.properties (empty here), overview.procedure, overview.notes, overview.sample, overview.siblings. In the side column, overview.location comes straight after the citation: the measurement's own map, else its sample's when the viewer may open the sample and it has a location. What the registry says about the type opens from the type badge in the header.
