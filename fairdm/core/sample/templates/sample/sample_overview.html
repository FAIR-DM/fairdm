{% extends "overview/page.html" %} {% load i18n humanize fairdm thumbnail %} {% comment %} The sample's own page, drawn from the base Sample model alone. A physical sample is understood through what it is, where it came from and its history: collected, prepared, stored, perhaps destroyed. The header says what it is and where it is now. The wide column gives the type's own properties, the notes, the history and what was measured on it. Beside it: how to cite it, where it was taken, its facts, its related samples and its people. The context is gathered by the sample `Overview` plugin. A sample type adds its own content by providing `/_overview.html`, which extends this template and fills its blocks. `{{ block.super }}` adds to a block and keeps what is already there: {% extends "sample/sample_overview.html" %} {% block overview.badges %}{{ block.super }}…{% endblock %} {% block overview.properties %} {% endblock %} Sample blocks in the wide column, in order: overview.properties (empty here, the block a type fills first), overview.notes, overview.history, overview.measurements. In the facts column, in order: overview.cite, overview.location (the map), overview.details, overview.samples (related samples), overview.people.
