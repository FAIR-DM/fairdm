A physical sample is understood through its history: collected, prepared, stored, perhaps destroyed. The page puts that history back together, then shows what was measured on the specimen, where it came from and what it is related to. The anatomy and the facts column come from `overview/page.html`; the context is gathered by `fairdm/core/sample/overview.py`.
