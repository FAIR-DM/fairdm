{% comment %} — daisyUI `progress` bar. A bar alone says nothing to a screen reader or to someone who can't judge its length, so `label` is required: say what is measured and how far along it is, in words, and show the same words beside the bar. Attributes: value – current amount max – total (default 100) label – accessible name, e.g. "Year 2 of 4" variant – primary, secondary, accent, info, success, warning, error class – extra classes on the Candidate for django-mvp. {% endcomment %}
