FairDM#

FairDM is an open-source web framework for building research data portals that follow the FAIR data principles. A portal records projects, datasets, samples and measurements, the people and organizations credited on them, and who may see and change each record. These pages are split by who is reading, so start with the entry that matches what you do.

Portal developer#

Developers is for you if you build a research data portal on FairDM and deploy it. It covers defining sample and measurement types, configuring a portal, extending its pages and running it in production.

Portal administrator#

Administrators is for you if you run a portal that already exists. It covers the people and roles, the review of content and the checks on a portal’s configuration.

Portal user#

Users is for you if you add, edit and publish research data in a portal. It follows the sections a portal shows in its navigation.
