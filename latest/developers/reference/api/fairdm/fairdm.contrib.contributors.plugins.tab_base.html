    fairdm.contrib.contributors.plugins.tab_base¶

    What every view of a record’s Contributors tab shares.

    People are listed in the main column and organizations beside them. Each row has one actions control that opens a sheet. Adding, editing and reordering happen in sheets, and every change answers with the whole region drawn again, so both columns and their counts always agree. The changes themselves are made by Crediting.

    people()¶

    Return the people on the record in their order, with what each row shows.

    organizations(people)¶

    Return every organization on the record in the order the tab shows them.

    An organization is on the tab when it is listed with roles or is the affiliation of someone listed.

    Args: people: The rows from people, whose affiliations count.

    Returns: One row per organization: the affiliations of the people who lead the record first, then organizations that lead it themselves, then the organizations credited directly in their order, then those that are only an affiliation, by name.

    in_default_order(rows, people)¶

    Order the organizations and give each the badges it shows.

    The affiliations of the people who lead the record come first, in the order those people are credited, then organizations that are Project Leader themselves, and all of them show Project Leader. An organization people are credited from, with no roles of its own, shows Project Member. One with roles of its own keeps them.

    Args: rows: The organization rows, listed ones first, then affiliations by name. people: The rows from people.

    Returns: The rows in their default order, each with badges, the role labels it shows.

    access_from_above(people)¶

    Return who holds access from a record above without being credited here.

    Args: people: The rows from people.

    Returns: One entry per person with their level label and the record it comes from, none for anyone who cannot see levels.

    property can_change¶

    Whether the viewer may change the record’s contributors.

    region_context(toast=None)¶

    Return everything the region draws.

    region_response(toast=None, close_sheet=True)¶

    Answer with the region drawn again, and close the open sheet.

    Args: toast: The notice to show, with its text, optionally tone and an undo. close_sheet: Whether the answer tells the page to close the open sheet.

    refusal(error)¶

    Answer a refused change with the region and the reason.

    sheet_response(template, context, title, lead='')¶

    Answer with a sheet for the sheet slot, and open it.

    step_response(template, context, chrome=True)¶

    Answer with the next step of the open sheet: its body, and with chrome its foot.

    Args: template: The step’s template. context: What it draws. chrome: Whether the answer also replaces the sheet’s foot and head, out of band. A list of search results answers without it.

    static head_for(contributor, new_name=None, organization=False)¶

    Return the sheet’s title on a step about one person or organization.

    Args: contributor: The person or organization, or None for one not yet in the portal. new_name: For a person not yet in the portal, their first_name and last_name; for an organization, its name. organization: Whether a contributor not yet in the portal is an organization.

    static foot(context)¶

    Return the sheet foot’s buttons, from the step’s context.

    search_registry(call, *args, **kwargs)¶

    Ask a registry for the signed-in person, counting it against their limit.

    Args: call: The registry method to call.

    Returns: (answer, problem): the answer, or None and "limited" when the person has searched too often, or "unavailable" when the registry could not be asked.

    registry_refusal(registry, problem)¶

    Answer a change that could not be made because a registry could not be asked.

    Args: registry: The registry’s name, shown in the message. problem: limited when the user searched too fast, otherwise the registry could not be reached.

    offered_roles()¶

    Return the roles this kind of record offers.

    locked_roles(held)¶

    Return the pks of the roles ticked and not removable.

    Project Member is kept for anyone being added and for anyone who holds it.

    Args: held: The pks of the roles the contributor holds now, or None when they are being added.

    chosen_roles(held)¶

    Return the roles ticked in the posted form, limited to the offered ones.

    Args: held: The pks of the roles the contributor holds now, or None when they are being added.

    static held_by(credit)¶

    Return the pks of the roles a contribution holds, or None when it counts as new.

    An organization listed with no roles of its own is there as someone’s affiliation and shows Project Member, so it is treated as just added.

    Args: credit: The contribution, or None when the contributor is being added.

    role_context(credit)¶

    Return the roles to offer, the ones ticked and the ones that can’t be unticked.

    Args: credit: The contribution being edited, or None when the contributor is being added.

    affiliation_choices(person)¶

    Return the organizations to credit a person from: theirs first, then every other one.

    Args: person: The person, or None for someone not yet in the portal.

    Returns: (theirs, others): theirs is a list of (organization, current) for the organizations on the person’s profile, current ones first, and others every other organization by name.
