    Home page¶

    The address / shows two pages. A signed-in user gets a dashboard (fairdm/dashboard.html). Everyone else gets the landing page (fairdm/landing.html), which this page describes: its sections, the template blocks that hold them and the view that feeds them. What a visitor sees on the screen is described in the home screen. The settings that change the page are described in Settings: HOME_PAGE_CONFIG, PORTAL_DESCRIPTION and ACCOUNT_ALLOW_REGISTRATION.

    The landing page is built from daisy-cotton-ext page blocks, whose stylesheet FairDM links for you. The view is fairdm.views.generic.FairDMHomeView, routed at / in fairdm.conf.urls.

    Sections¶

    The page has no navigation bar, so the hero starts at the top of the window. The sections come in this order. Each is a template block, a component and a context entry the view provides. A section with nothing to show draws nothing.

    Two more blocks complete the template:

    Block

    landing.header

    Empty. Fill it to put a navigation bar above the hero, for example <c-app.header />.

    landing.footer fills the shell’s app.footer block, and landing.header fills its app.header block.

    What a section may show¶

    Every method that feeds a section returns only what a visitor who has not signed in may open. A private dataset, a public dataset inside a private project, and the samples and measurements of a dataset that is not published are never counted or named.

    FairDMHomeView¶

    Attributes¶

    Attribute

    Default

    What it limits

    latest_limit

    3

    Datasets in the latest datasets section, and projects in the new projects section.

    record_type_limit

    5

    Sample types, and measurement types, listed before the rest are counted.

    people_limit

    8

    People in the community section.

    organization_limit

    6

    Organizations named in the community section.

    hero_defaults

    a dictionary

    The hero’s content where HOME_PAGE_CONFIG leaves a key out: logo (True), eyebrow, title, lead and image (None).

    landing_template_name

    fairdm/landing.html

    The template a visitor gets.

    dashboard_template_name

    fairdm/dashboard.html

    The template a signed-in user gets.

    Methods¶

    Method

    Returns

    Feeds

    get_landing_context(context)

    The context with every entry below added.

    The landing template.

    get_portal_name()

    The name saved in the portal identity, else SITE_NAME.

    hero and about

    get_hero()

    A dictionary of eyebrow, title, lead (with {site_name} filled in, empty where the setting is False), image (an address, or empty) and logo.

    hero

    get_about()

    A dictionary of name, description (the saved one, else PORTAL_DESCRIPTION), keywords, authority and authority_url.

    about

    get_counts()

    A list of dictionaries with title, icon and value for public datasets, samples, measurements, people and organizations, leaving out any that is zero.

    portal_counts

    get_latest_datasets()

    At most latest_limit public datasets, most recently modified first, loaded with with_list_data().

    latest_datasets

    get_latest_projects()

    At most latest_limit public projects, most recently added first, loaded with with_list_data() and with_contributors().

    latest_projects

    get_record_types(base, models)

    A dictionary of shown (at most record_type_limit types, each with name, description, count and url, most public records first) and more. base is Sample or Measurement.

    sample_types, measurement_types

    get_people()

    At most people_limit people, credited on the most records first.

    people

    get_organizations()

    At most organization_limit organizations, credited on the most records first.

    organizations

    The view also adds people_count (the number of people the portal lists) and allow_registration (the ACCOUNT_ALLOW_REGISTRATION setting).
