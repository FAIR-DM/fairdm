Home page#

The address / shows two pages. A signed-in user gets a dashboard (fairdm/dashboard.html). Everyone else gets the landing page (fairdm/landing.html), which this page describes: its sections, the template blocks that hold them and the view that feeds them. What a visitor sees on the screen is described in the home screen. The settings that change the page are described in Settings: HOME_PAGE_CONFIG, PORTAL_DESCRIPTION and ACCOUNT_ALLOW_REGISTRATION.

The landing page is built from daisy-cotton-ext page blocks, whose stylesheet FairDM links for you. The view is fairdm.views.generic.FairDMHomeView, routed at / in fairdm.conf.urls.

What a section may show#

Every method that feeds a section returns only what a visitor who has not signed in may open. A private dataset, a public dataset inside a private project, and the samples and measurements of a dataset that is not published are never counted or named.

FairDMHomeView#

Attributes#

Attribute

Default

What it limits

latest_limit

3

Datasets in the latest datasets section, and projects in the new projects section.

record_type_limit

5

Sample types, and measurement types, listed before the rest are counted.

people_limit

8

People in the community section.

organization_limit

6

Organizations named in the community section.

hero_defaults

a dictionary

The hero’s content where HOME_PAGE_CONFIG leaves a key out: logo (True), eyebrow, title, lead and image (None).

landing_template_name

fairdm/landing.html

The template a visitor gets.

dashboard_template_name

fairdm/dashboard.html

The template a signed-in user gets.
