# How to approve a pending affiliation

Approving a pending affiliation changes its type from **Pending** to **Member**, which gives the person the standing of a verified member. You do this after you have checked that the person belongs to the organization, one affiliation at a time, because no bulk approve action exists.

## Before you start

- You can approve from the **Affiliations** screen if you are a superuser or an owner of the organization. That screen lists only the affiliations of organizations you own, and a superuser sees all of them.
- If you hold the Community Manager role without owning the organization, approve from the person's own change form, as described after the steps. The Portal Administrator role holds no right over affiliations.
- Approving to **Admin** or **Owner** rather than **Member** needs the right to manage the organization. Only an owner or a superuser has it.

## Steps

1. In the administration interface, open **Community** and select **Affiliations**.

2. In the **Type** filter on the right, choose **Pending**. The list shows each pending affiliation with its person and organization.

3. Open the affiliation you have verified.

4. Change **Type** to **Member** and select **Save**.

To approve from a person's change form instead, open the person under **Community** → **Users**, change **Type** in the affiliation row to **Member** and select **Save**.

## The result

The affiliation no longer appears under the **Pending** filter. The person holds the type **Member** for that organization. To reject an affiliation, delete it from the same screen, or leave it as **Pending**.

No permission is written when you approve, because an organization's management right follows the owner affiliation only. See [How contributor accounts and credits work](../../explanation/how-contributor-accounts-and-credits-work.md).

The screen is described in [Affiliations](../reference/affiliations.md).
