# How to add a person or organization

Adding a contributor credits a person or an organization on a record. Do it when someone took part in the work, or when an organization funded or hosted it.

## Before you start

You need to manage the record, which means holding the **Can manage** level on it or on the record above it. A superuser cannot be credited, so the portal adds nobody when you choose one.

## Steps

1. Open the record's page and choose its **Contributors** tab.
2. Choose **Add person** to credit a person, or **Add organization** to credit an organization.
3. Find the contributor on one of three tabs. Use the first that fits.
    - **Already in the portal**: type part of the name and choose **Search**, then choose the match. A contributor already credited on the record is marked and cannot be added again.
    - **Find by ORCID** for a person, or **Find in ROR** for an organization: search by name or by identifier, then choose the match. The portal makes a profile from the registry's record. A person made this way has no account and can claim the profile later by signing in with ORCID. A profile that already holds the identifier is used instead of a second one.
    - **New person** or **New organization**: enter the details by hand. For a person, enter a **Given name** and a **Family name**. For an organization, enter a **Name** and, if you want, a **City**, a **Country** and a **Website**. Give the country by name or by its two-letter code.
4. For a person, choose the organization they are credited from on this record. See [How to change the organization a person is credited from](change-the-organization-a-person-is-credited-from.md) for what the choice means.
5. Choose **Add to this** followed by the record's kind. For an organization found in the portal, choose **Add**.
6. On the **Edit a contributor** screen that opens, [set the contributor's roles](set-a-contributors-roles.md) and, for a person, their level. Choose **Save**.

If a person with the same name is already in the portal, the **New person** tab lists those profiles first. Choose one of them if it is who you mean, so their work stays on one profile. Otherwise choose **Make a new profile anyway and add**. An organization with the same name as one in the portal is never made twice.

If the registry cannot be reached, the tab says so. The other two tabs still work.

## The result

The contributor is credited on the record, last in their list. A person starts at **Can view** with no roles, so on a private record they can open it and cannot change it. An organization holds no access. Nobody is emailed.

The organization you chose for a person is listed under Organizations on the record too, once, however many people are credited from it.

The screens are described on [Contributors](contributors-tab.md). A person's account and how a profile is claimed are explained in [How contributor accounts and credits work](../../explanation/how-contributor-accounts-and-credits-work.md).
