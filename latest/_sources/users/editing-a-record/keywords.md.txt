# Keywords

The Keywords screen sets the free keywords a record is found under: words a person types, with no vocabulary behind them. Projects, datasets, samples and measurements all have it, and it is the same screen on all four.

## How to reach it

Open the record's page, choose **Manage**, then **Keywords**. The address is the record's own address followed by `keywords/`:

| Record | Address |
|---|---|
| Project | `/projects/<portal ID>/keywords/` |
| Dataset | `/datasets/<portal ID>/keywords/` |
| Sample | `/samples/<portal ID>/keywords/` |
| Measurement | `/measurement/<portal ID>/keywords/` |

You see the entry when you may change the record. See [How access to a record is decided](../../explanation/how-access-to-a-record-is-decided.md) for who that is, and for what you are told when you ask for the screen without that right.

## The field

The screen has one field, **Free keywords**, with the help text "Words this record should be found under." It shows the free keywords the record already carries. Type a word and press Enter to add it, and remove a word to take it off. The field accepts words that are not yet in the portal.

### When the record has none

The field is empty. Saving with the field empty removes every free keyword the record carries.

## Keywords from a vocabulary

Keywords chosen from a controlled vocabulary are a separate set. This screen does not edit them, and saving leaves any the record already carries as they are. Those are the keywords shown as badges under the record's name on its page, so a free keyword does not appear there.

## Saving

Choose **Save & continue** or **Save & continue editing**. Saving replaces the record's free keywords with what is in the field. You arrive on the record's page, which shows "Saved" followed by the record's name.

## What you can do here

- Add, remove or clear free keywords, then save.
- Leave without saving by going back to the record's page.
- Change the record's other details: [Descriptions](descriptions.md), [Key dates](key-dates.md) and [Identifiers](identifiers.md).
