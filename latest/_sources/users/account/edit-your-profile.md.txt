# How to edit your profile

This guide changes what your public profile says about you: your name, photo, biography, links and languages. You do it from the portal, without asking an administrator.

## Before you start

You must be signed in. Your email address, password and connected sign-ins are not part of your profile. Change those in the [Account Center](account-centre.md).

## Steps

1. Open your profile from the [People](../community/people.md) screen, by selecting your name.
2. Select **Edit profile** in the header.
3. Change the fields you want to change. The [Edit profile](edit-profile.md) screen lists them all.
4. To add a photo, choose a JPEG, PNG or WebP image in **Photo**. To remove one, tick the clear box beside it.
5. To add links, type one web address on each line of **Links**, each beginning `http://` or `https://`.
6. Select **Save**.

## The result

You return to your profile with the message **Your changes were saved.** The new details show in the header, in **About** and in **Links**. The **Your profile** checklist counts the items that are in place. See [Your profile](your-profile.md).

If a field cannot be saved, nothing is saved. The field says what is wrong, and everything else you typed stays in the form. Correct the field and select **Save** again. Select **Cancel** to leave without changing anything.
