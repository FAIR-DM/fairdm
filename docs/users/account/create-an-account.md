# How to create an account

This guide makes you an account on a portal, so you can create records and edit the ones you are given access to. You do this once for each portal.

## Before you start

You need an email address you can read, because the portal sends a message there that you must open. If the portal says sign up is closed, ask its administrators to create an account for you.

## Steps

1. Open the portal and select **Create account** under the buttons at the top of the [Home](../home.md) screen.
2. On the [Sign up](sign-up.md) screen, type your email address in **Email**.
3. Type a password in **Password**, and the same password in **Password (again)**.
4. Type your given name in **First name** and your family name in **Last name**.
5. Select **Sign Up**.
6. Open the email the portal sent to your address and select the link in it. If you cannot find the message, check your spam folder.

## Sign up with ORCID instead

If you have an ORCID iD, you can sign up with it and skip the password:

1. On the [Sign up](sign-up.md) screen, select **ORCID**.
2. On the page headed **Sign In Via ORCID**, select **Continue**, then sign in at ORCID and authorise the portal.
3. If the portal shows a page headed **Sign Up**, check your email address and **Display name**, then select **Sign Up**.

Signing up with ORCID also links any profile already made for you. See [How to claim your profile with ORCID](claim-your-profile-with-orcid.md).

## The result

After step 5 the portal shows a page headed **Verify Your Email Address**. After you follow the link in step 6 you are signed in and arrive on the [Home](../home.md) screen, headed **My Dashboard**, with a greeting such as `Welcome, Ada!`

Next, [Create your first dataset](../tutorial.md), or [edit your profile](edit-your-profile.md) so colleagues can recognise you.
