# Projects

The Projects screen lists the projects in the portal as cards, with search, sorting and filters. Open it from **Projects** in the navigation menu. Anyone can open it, signed in or not, and everyone sees the same list: the projects whose visibility is Public. A private project never appears here, not even for the people who work on it. They reach their private projects from the project's own page. See [How access to a record is decided](../../explanation/how-access-to-a-record-is-decided.md) for the reasoning.

## The toolbar

The toolbar sits above the list and has these controls:

- **Search** matches the project's name, its portal ID and the values of its identifiers.
- **Status** keeps the projects at the stages you tick: Concept, Planning, In progress, Complete or Searching for collaborators.
- **Sort** orders the list. The choices are Default order, Name (A-Z), Name (Z-A), Date created (oldest first) and Date created (newest first). Default order puts the most recently updated project first.
- **Filters** opens a panel with the remaining filters.
- **Add** opens [Create a project](create-project.md). Only signed-in people see it.

The panel holds these filters:

- **Has image** keeps the projects that have a cover image.
- **Owner** keeps the projects owned by the organizations you choose.
- **Tag** keeps the projects carrying a tag.
- **Contributor** keeps the projects the people you choose are credited on.
- **Keywords** keeps the projects carrying the keywords you tick. When the portal names keyword vocabularies, there is one filter for each vocabulary, labelled with the vocabulary's name. See [settings](../../developers/reference/settings.md).

Filters combine, so a project must match all of them. Under the toolbar a line gives the number of projects that match and, when you have chosen a sort, the order the list is in.

## The project cards

Each card is one link to the project's page. A card shows:

- the project's image, or an icon when it has none
- a **Project** badge and a badge for its status
- the number of public datasets in it, or **No datasets**
- the project's name
- the first people credited on it, and the name of the owner organization
- a short piece of the abstract
- the project's keywords
- the portal ID, with a button that copies it
- when the project was last updated

The list shows 25 projects to a page, with page links under the last card.

## When the list is empty

With nothing to show, the screen shows the heading **There's nothing here yet**. A signed-in person also sees the line "You haven't added any records yet. Click the button below to get started." and an **Add new** button.

When a search or filter matches nothing, the heading reads **No projects match**, with the line "Try other words or fewer filters." and a **Clear all** button.
