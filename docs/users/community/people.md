# People

The People screen lists everyone the portal knows by name: the people who work on its records, whether or not they have an account. It is the place to find a colleague's profile.

You reach it by opening **Community** in the navigation menu and choosing **People**, or by selecting **Meet the people** on the [Home](../home.md) screen. Anyone can open it, signed in or not.

The list includes people who have never signed in, because the portal creates a profile for someone credited before they join. It leaves out superuser accounts, which exist only to run the portal.

## The toolbar

Along the top of the list are:

- the heading **People**, with a count such as `12 users`
- a search box headed **Contributor Name**, with a **Search** button
- an **Active** switch, which limits the list to people whose account is active
- a **Staff** switch, which limits the list to staff accounts
- a **Filters** button

Below the toolbar, each search or filter you have applied shows as a button. Selecting one removes it. When you have applied more than one, **Clear all** removes them together. Once a search or filter is applied, the count reads `n results` or **No results**.

## The filters panel

The **Filters** button opens a panel beside the list with three text boxes:

- **City**
- **Country**
- **Affiliation**

**Affiliation** matches part of the name of an organization a person is affiliated with, ignoring capitals. The panel has an **Apply** button, and a **Clear** button once a filter in it is set.

## The cards

Each person is a card. Cards are laid out two across on a medium screen and three across on a wide one. A card shows:

- the person's photo, or their initials when they have none
- their name, linked to their profile, followed by an ORCID icon when they have an ORCID iD
- the organization they are primarily affiliated with, when they have one
- the place of that organization, when it has one
- a badge for each portal role they hold, such as **Community Manager**

The list shows 25 people to a page. When there are more, the foot of the screen shows `Showing` a range `of` the total, with page numbers. On a narrow screen it shows two buttons and the page count instead.

## When it is empty

A portal with nobody to list shows an icon and the heading **There's nothing here yet**. The screen has no button for adding a person.

When a search or filter matches nobody, the heading reads **No users match**, with the line **Try other words or fewer filters.** and a **Clear all** button.

## What you can do here

- Open a profile by selecting a name. See [Your profile](../account/your-profile.md) for what a profile shows.
- Find your own card and edit your profile. See [How to edit your profile](../account/edit-your-profile.md).
- Take over a profile that was made for you. See [How to claim your profile with ORCID](../account/claim-your-profile-with-orcid.md).
- Credit someone on a record you manage. See [How to add a person or organization](../contributors/add-a-person-or-organization.md).
