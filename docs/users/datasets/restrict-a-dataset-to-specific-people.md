# How to restrict a dataset to specific people

Restricting a dataset closes its page to everyone except the people you choose. Do it when a dataset's description should be seen only by one research team, for example while the work is under embargo.

## Before you start

You need the **Can manage** level on the dataset or on the project above it. Only managers see the **Visibility** field. See [How access to a record is decided](../../explanation/how-access-to-a-record-is-decided.md).

## Steps

1. Open [the dataset page](dataset.md). Open the **Manage** menu and choose **Edit details**.
2. Under **Visibility**, choose **Private**.
3. Select **Save & continue**. The dataset page shows a **Private** badge.
4. Open the **Contributors** tab. Read who is listed and what each person may do. Below the lists, the **Access from the project** card names anyone who can open the dataset because of what they hold on its project. Those people are changed on the project, through its **Manage there** button.
5. For each person who should have access and is not listed, select **Add person** and choose them. They join with the **Can view** level. [How to let a colleague into a private record](../contributors/let-a-colleague-into-a-private-record.md) shows the choices.
6. For each person who should change the dataset, select **Edit** beside their name, choose **Can edit** or **Can manage**, and select **Save**.
7. Ask one of the people you listed to open the dataset from a link.

## The result

The dataset opens only for these people:

- anyone listed on the dataset, at any level
- anyone listed on its project
- people who hold a portal role that can see every dataset, such as Data Curator

Everyone else, signed in or not, is told the dataset does not exist. The dataset also leaves the [Datasets](datasets.md) screen for them.

A level on the project reaches every dataset in it. When one team works on all of a project's datasets, list the team on the project instead of on each dataset.

Making a dataset private restricts its page. It does not change whether its samples and measurements are published. Those are hidden from everyone outside the team until the dataset is published.
