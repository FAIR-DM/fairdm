# How to add a person or organization

Adding a contributor credits a person or an organization on a record. Do it when someone took part in the work, or when an organization funded or hosted it.

## Before you start

You need to manage the record, which means holding the **Can manage** level on it or on the record above it. A superuser cannot be credited, so the portal never offers one.

## Steps

1. Open the record's page and choose its **Contributors** tab.
2. Choose **Add** beside **People** to credit a person, or beside **Organizations** to credit an organization.
3. Type a name. The portal's own matches are listed first.
    - Choose a match from the portal. Someone already credited on the record is not listed.
    - For a person, a name or an ORCID iD the portal does not have is a **New** row. Choosing it asks ORCID who it knows under that name. Choose the person you mean, or add them by name alone and confirm their given names and family name. A person added from ORCID gets a profile made from their ORCID record, with no account, and can claim it later by signing in with ORCID. A profile that already holds the iD is used instead of a second one.
    - For an organization, a **New** row asks ROR the same way. Choose the organization you mean, or add it by name alone.
4. For a person, choose the organization they are credited from on this record. The organizations on their ORCID record are listed first. See [How to change the organization a person is credited from](change-the-organization-a-person-is-credited-from.md) for what the choice means.
5. Tick the contributor's roles, then choose **Add**. On a project or a dataset, Project Member is ticked and stays ticked.

If a registry cannot be reached, or you have searched a lot in a minute, the step says so and nobody is added.

## The result

The contributor is credited on the record, last in their list, and a notice confirms it with **Undo** for removals. A person starts at **Can view**, so on a private record they can open it and cannot change it. An organization holds no access. Nobody is emailed. To give a person more access, [edit them](let-a-colleague-into-a-private-record.md).

The organization you chose for a person is listed under Organizations on the record too, once, however many people are credited from it.

The screens are described on [Contributors](contributors-tab.md). A person's account and how a profile is claimed are explained in [How contributor accounts and credits work](../../explanation/how-contributor-accounts-and-credits-work.md).
