# Contributor roles

A role says how a person or organization is credited on a record: what they did for it. Roles
are credit only. They never change what anyone may do on the record.

Each kind of record offers its own roles. Everyone added to a project or a dataset is a Project Member, and the role cannot be taken off them. The
meanings below are the definitions in the portal's contributor roles vocabulary.

## Project

| Role | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Creator | The person or organization responsible for creating the project. |
| Project Leader | The person who led the project. |
| Project Member | A member of the project. |
| Project Manager | The person who managed the project. |
| Contact Person | The person to contact for information about the project. |
| Other | A role that does not fit into any of the other categories. |

## Dataset

| Role | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Creator | The person or organization responsible for creating the dataset. |
| Contact Person | The person to contact for information about the dataset. |
| Data Collector | The person who collected the data. |
| Data Curator | The person who curated the data. |
| Data Manager | The person who managed the data. |
| Editor | The person who edited the data. |
| Producer | The person who produced the data. |
| Related Person | A person who is related to the dataset. |
| Researcher | The person who conducted the research. |
| Project Leader | The person who led the project the dataset belongs to. |
| Project Manager | The person who managed the project the dataset belongs to. |
| Project Member | A member of the project the dataset belongs to. |
| Supervisor | The person who supervised the work. |
| Work Package Leader | The person who led the work package. |
| Rights Holder | The person or organization owning the rights to the dataset. |
| Other | A role that does not fit into any of the other categories. |

## Sample

| Role | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Collector | The person who collected the sample. |
| Preparer | The person who prepared the sample for analysis. |
| Archivist | The person who stored the sample. |
| Destroyer | The person who destroyed the sample. |
| Restorer | The person who restored the sample. |

## Measurement

| Role | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Preparation | Carried out the procedures and preparations before the measurement, so the conditions for accurate and reliable data were met. |
| Collection | Acquired the data or measurements through established methods or protocols. |
| Support | Provided the tools, equipment or expertise the measurement needed. |

## Roles and citations

Roles decide who a record's citation names. A project's and a dataset's citation names the people
with the Creator role, a sample's names its Collectors, and a measurement's names the people with
the Collection role. See [How to set a contributor's roles](../users/contributors/set-a-contributors-roles.md).
