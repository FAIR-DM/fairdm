# Editing your profile

Your profile is the public page other people reach from a credit on a project or dataset. You change
it yourself, from the portal, without asking an administrator.

## Open the editing page

Sign in and open your own profile. There are three ways to reach the editing page:

- Choose **Edit profile** in the header of your profile.
- Choose **Fix** next to the photo, biography or links item on your profile checklist. You arrive
  at the field that item asks for.
- Choose **Write your biography** in the About card when you have not written one yet.

Only you see these links while your account is active. Nobody else is offered the editing page for
your profile, and a request for its address by anyone else is refused. The one exception is a
profile nobody can sign in to, such as one nobody has claimed or one whose account has been
deactivated: the portal's Community Managers can correct it until the person has an active account.

## What you can change

The page starts with your name, then groups the other fields under two headings: About you and
Elsewhere online. The photo
has a column of its own beside them on a wide screen.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Given name and family name | The two parts of your name as citations use them. |
| Display name | The name you are publicly known by. It is required, and it appears on every credit that names you. |
| Alternative names | Other names you publish under, one per line. |
| Photo | A JPEG, PNG or WebP image. The page names the size limit. To remove your photo, tick the clear box next to it. Your initials are shown in its place. |
| Biography | A few lines about your research. Markdown is supported. |
| Links | Web addresses of your other profiles, one per line. Each must start with `http://` or `https://`. |
| Languages | The languages you work in. Choose as many as you need. |

Empty lines are dropped, and an entry you type twice is kept once.

## Save your changes

Choose **Save**. You return to your profile, the portal confirms that the change was saved, and the
checklist counts the items that are now in place.

If something cannot be saved, nothing is saved. The field at fault says what is wrong, and
everything else you typed stays in the form so you can correct it and try again. Choose **Cancel**,
or leave the page, to change nothing.

## What is managed elsewhere

The editing page does not change your email address, password or connected sign-ins. Those are in
your account centre, which the editing page links to. Your ORCID iD is
connected by signing in with ORCID, and your affiliations and portal roles are set by the people
who run the portal.
