# Editing an organization's profile

An organization's page is what visitors read when a project or dataset names it, and what shows
under the name of everyone affiliated with it. The people who keep its record change it themselves,
from the portal, without asking an administrator.

## Who can edit it

The organization's owner and its administrators, as long as their affiliation has not ended and
their account is active, and the portal's Community Managers, who may edit any organization,
including one with no owner or administrators. A Community Manager who does not keep the
organization is offered a single **Edit details** button in the header in place of the **Manage**
menu, and does not see its checklist. An ordinary member, someone with no affiliation to the
organization and a person who only holds the Data Curator role are not offered the editing page, and
a request for its address by any of them is refused.

## Open the editing page

Sign in and open the organization. There are three ways to reach the editing page:

- Open the **Manage** menu in the header and choose **Edit details**. The menu's other entries are
  not available yet. A Community Manager who does not keep the organization chooses the **Edit
  details** button instead.
- Choose **Fix** next to the logo, type, city and country, description or website item on the
  organization's checklist. You arrive at the field that item asks for.
- Choose **Write a description** in the About card when the organization has none yet.

The checklist item for the ROR identifier stays as it is. The editing page does not change it.

## What you can change

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Logo | A JPEG, PNG or WebP image. The page names the size limit. To remove the logo, tick the clear box next to it. The organization's initials are shown in its place. |
| Name | The name the organization is publicly known by. It is required, and it appears wherever the organization is credited. |
| Alternative names | Other names the organization is known by, one per line. |
| Type | The kind of institution, such as education or funder. |
| Part of | The organization this one belongs to. Search for it by name, or clear the field to make this organization part of nothing. |
| City and country | Where the organization is based. |
| Description | A few lines about what the organization does. Markdown is supported. |
| Website | The organization's own web address. |
| Other links | Web addresses of other pages about the organization, one per line. |

Every web address must start with `http://` or `https://`. Empty lines are dropped, and an
address you type twice is kept once.

The website and the other links are stored together as one list, with the website first. If you
clear the website and save, the first of the other links becomes the website the next time you open
the page.

### Choosing the organization this one is part of

An organization cannot be part of itself, or part of one of its own sub-organizations at any depth.
If you choose one of those, nothing is saved and the field says why. Clearing the field leaves the
organization's own sub-organizations exactly as they were.

## Save your changes

Choose **Save**. You return to the organization's page, the portal confirms that the change was
saved, and the checklist counts the items that are now in place. A changed name or logo shows
wherever the organization is named, on the projects it owns and on the profiles of its members.

If something cannot be saved, nothing is saved. The field at fault says what is wrong, and everything
else you typed stays in the form so you can correct it and try again. Choose **Cancel**, or leave
the page, to change nothing.

## What is managed elsewhere

The editing page does not change the organization's ROR identifier, its members or its owner.
