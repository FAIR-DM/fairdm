# Delete

The delete page removes a sample permanently, along with its descriptions, dates and identifiers.
Deletion cannot be undone.

You reach it from the **Manage** menu on the sample's own page. It is the last entry, set apart from
the others by a divider.

## Who can open it

Anyone holding permission to delete the sample, which a manager of the sample's dataset has.
Someone who may only edit the sample sees the other Manage entries and no Delete, and is refused if
they type the address.

## What goes with it

Before you confirm, the page lists the rows that go with the sample.

## Confirming a deletion

To delete a sample you must type its name exactly as it appears. Surrounding spaces are ignored, but
the rest of the name must match. **Back** abandons the deletion and returns you to the sample.

## When deletion is refused

A sample cannot be deleted while measurements were made on it. The page says so, lists the
measurements that are in the way and offers no way to confirm. A measurement can sit in a dataset
you hold no access to, so the page names only the measurements you may see, by name or portal ID,
and counts the rest. Remove those measurements before the sample can be deleted.

## After deletion

You are taken to the dataset the sample belonged to, or to the dataset listing if you may not open
that dataset. A message tells you the sample was deleted.
