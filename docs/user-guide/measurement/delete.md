# Delete

The delete page removes a measurement permanently, along with its descriptions, dates and
identifiers. Deletion cannot be undone.

You reach it from the **Manage** menu on the measurement's own page. It is the last entry, set apart
from the others by a divider.

## Who can open it

Anyone holding permission to delete the measurement, which a manager of the measurement's dataset
has. Someone who may only edit the measurement sees the other Manage entries and no Delete, and is
refused if they type the address.

## What goes with it

Before you confirm, the page lists the rows that go with the measurement.

## Confirming a deletion

To delete a measurement you must type its name exactly as it appears. A measurement with no name is
confirmed by typing its portal ID. Surrounding spaces are ignored, but the rest must match.
**Back** abandons the deletion and returns you to the measurement.

## After deletion

You are taken to the dataset the measurement belonged to, or to the dataset listing if you may not
open that dataset. A message tells you the measurement was deleted.
